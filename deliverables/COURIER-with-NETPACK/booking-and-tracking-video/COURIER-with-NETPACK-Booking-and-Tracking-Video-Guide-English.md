# COURIER with NETPACK — Booking and Detailed Tracking Video Guide

This video uses real application screens with isolated demonstration data. It does not use slide presentations and does not change staging or production information.

## Included booking flows

1. Domestic shipment booking — service tier, pickup contact, delivery contact, Nepal-ready address, weight, dimensions and parcel description.
2. International shipment booking — sender, recipient, country, postal code, weight, dimensions, service level, signature and insurance conditions.
3. E-commerce/on-demand booking — merchant, customer, product value, delivery window and special instructions.
4. Grocery Box — product selection, box weight management, optimisation and quotation.

## Detailed tracking shown

- Domestic: booking created, validation, pickup assignment, pickup verification, origin-hub scan, manifest dispatch, transit scan, destination-hub arrival, out for delivery, recipient verification and POD completion.
- International: booking, compliance review, chargeable weight, pickup, HAWB gateway scan, export departure, international transit, import arrival, customs review, customs release, destination depot, out for delivery and signed delivery.
- E-commerce: order placement, rider assignment, pickup, GPS movement, delivery, COD collection and COD verification.

The domestic and international tracking demonstrations use logically ordered events one minute apart so each milestone and timestamp can be examined clearly. They are demonstration timestamps, not claims of real-world one-minute delivery.

## Privacy and accuracy

Public tracking hides exact addresses, phone numbers, payment details and documents. Precise rider location is restricted to the customer, merchant, assigned rider and authorized operations users. Live carrier, payment, messaging and routing integrations require approved credentials and production configuration.
